HealGym

A high-performance bodybuilding and diet platform designed to maximize results through personalization, data tracking, and gamification.
Tailors workout and diet plans to each user’s body type, age, weight, and goals.
Uses Artificial Intelligence to adjust exercise execution in real time, ensuring precision and avoiding generic routines.

Mission
Deliver the highest possible performance for every user by combining personalized training, proper nutrition, and data-driven insights — filling the gap where personal trainers cannot provide constant attention.

Features

Automatic creation of personalized bodybuilding workouts

Customized diet plans aligned with individual goals

AI-powered adjustment of exercise movements for precision and safety

Adaptation for different body types, ages, and experience levels

Gamification system focused on training days, lifted weight, and progression milestones

Real-time feedback and plan updates based on performance data

Full focus on individual needs to achieve optimal results

Philosophy
No great results without both proper training and proper diet. HealGym integrates both into a single, adaptive ecosystem.
